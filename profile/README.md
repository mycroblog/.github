### 🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳 Grow at your own pace

Plant it on a tiny VM with one binary and SQLite, or on a container that sleeps when nobody is around.
When your friends keep coming, let it grow into PostgreSQL, a job queue and as many web and worker processes as you
need. Same code, same data, no moving house. 🏡

### ✨ What's inside

- 🌐 **Federates** with Mastodon, Misskey, GoToSocial and more
- 🏠 **"This server" posts** that stay among your people and your followers
- 🎙️ **Voice rooms** to hang out and talk
- 💝 **Supporters**, so your members can help carry the server's costs
- 🛡️ **Moderation built in**, gentle enough for a small team
- 🧩 **Plugins and hooks**, so you can make it yours without forking
- 🗺️ **Translated from day one**
